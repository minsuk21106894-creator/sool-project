Drinking Etiquette
1 Pour for others, not yourself. Someone will pour for you in return.
2 Receive a drink with both hands, or support your pouring arm with the other hand.
3 Turn slightly away from elders when drinking. It's a sign of respect.
4 Never let a glass sit empty. Refill before it's fully drained.
5 "Anju" (drinking food) is essential. Koreans rarely drink without food.
